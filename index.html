NuvoraCore

NuvoraCore is a private desktop tool used by NuvoraStudio to produce and publish videos on its own YouTube channels (The Ellsworth Hour, Relatos do Fim do Mundo, Nico Sin Límites).

It runs only on the owner's computer. It uses the YouTube API Services to upload the channel's own videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, choose when a video becomes public, read the channel's own analytics and reply to comments. It is not offered to the public and does not access any other person's account.
