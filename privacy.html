NuvoraCore — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

NuvoraCore is a private desktop tool that runs on its owner's computer and manages only the owner's own YouTube channel.

YouTube API Services

NuvoraCore uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is accessed

With your permission (Google sign-in), NuvoraCore accesses only the YouTube channel you authorize: the channel's ID and name, and the videos NuvoraCore uploads for you (their upload and processing status, title, description, privacy, thumbnail and captions). It does not read comments, analytics, subscribers or any other account.

How it is used

Only to upload your own videos, set their title, description, privacy, thumbnail and captions, check their processing status, and make them public at the time you choose. The data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.

Where it is stored

Everything stays on your own computer: the access token and the uploaded videos' IDs and status are saved locally in the NuvoraCore folder. Nothing is sent to servers operated by NuvoraStudio (there are none). Disconnecting the channel in NuvoraCore deletes the token from the computer; local data can also be deleted at any time by deleting those files.

Cookies

NuvoraCore does not use cookies or tracking.

Revoking access

You can revoke NuvoraCore's access at any time with the Disconnect button in NuvoraCore (Channels screen), which revokes the token at Google, or at Google security settings → Third-party apps.
