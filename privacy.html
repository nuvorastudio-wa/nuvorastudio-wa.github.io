NuvoraCore — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

NuvoraCore is a private tool that runs on its owner's computer and manages only the owner's own YouTube channels.

YouTube API Services

NuvoraCore uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is accessed

With your permission (Google sign-in), NuvoraCore accesses the YouTube channel you authorize: channel and video information, video uploads, thumbnails, captions, comments on your own videos, and your channel's analytics reports.

How it is used

Only to upload and publish your own videos, update their metadata, reply to and moderate comments on your own videos, and show you performance reports. The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Where it is stored

Everything stays on your own computer: the access token and any reports are saved locally in the NuvoraCore folder. Nothing is sent to servers operated by NuvoraStudio (there are none). Local data can be deleted at any time by deleting those files.

Cookies

NuvoraCore does not use cookies or tracking.

Revoking access

You can revoke NuvoraCore's access at any time at Google security settings → Third-party apps.
